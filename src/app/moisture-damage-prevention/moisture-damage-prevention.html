What is Moisture Damage Prevention?

Moisture damage prevention focuses on controlling humidity levels inside packaging and transportation environments. It helps protect products from corrosion, mold growth, and deterioration caused by excess moisture.

Using desiccants, proper packaging materials, and humidity control methods ensures products remain dry and safe during storage and long-distance shipping.

Methods of Moisture Prevention

Use of Desiccants

Desiccants such as calcium chloride and magnesium chloride absorb excess humidity inside packaging.

Moisture Barrier Packaging

Special packaging materials prevent outside humidity from entering the product packaging.

Controlled Storage

Maintaining proper temperature and humidity conditions reduces moisture risks during storage.

Container Moisture Prevention

During ocean shipping, containers experience large temperature fluctuations that cause condensation and container rain.

Container desiccants are used to absorb excess humidity and prevent moisture from damaging cargo during transportation.
