Desiccants

Desiccants are moisture-absorbing materials used to control humidity and keep products dry inside sealed environments. They help protect goods from moisture-related damage such as corrosion, mold growth, and product deterioration.

Desiccants are widely used in packaging, storage, and shipping industries including electronics, pharmaceuticals, textiles, food, and chemicals to maintain product quality and shelf life.

Selecting the right desiccant depends on humidity levels, temperature conditions, storage duration, and the sensitivity of the product being protected.

Moisture Protection Solutions

Absorbent desiccants absorb moisture by chemically bonding water into the bulk of the desiccant material. They are slow acting but have high absorption capacity and remain active longer.

They are used to protect products from corrosion, mold growth, and moisture damage during storage and transportation.

These desiccants offer higher moisture absorption capacity than silica gel and clay, even in high-humidity conditions.

Absorbent desiccants help maintain product quality and ensure reliable moisture protection across the supply chain.

Magnesium Chloride Desiccant

Magnesium chloride desiccant is a powerful moisture absorber designed for industrial packaging, container shipping and warehouse storage protection. It is formulated using anhydrous magnesium chloride (MgCl₂) combined with a moisture-locking polymer to quickly capture humidity from the surrounding air.

When exposed to moisture, magnesium chloride reacts with water vapor and gradually converts from powder into a solid block. This process locks the moisture inside the desiccant structure, preventing leakage and ensuring safe and reliable moisture control during storage and transportation.

Adsorbent desiccants Adsorbent desiccants control moisture by attracting and holding water vapor on their surface through physical adsorption.

They are commonly used to protect products from humidity-related issues such as corrosion, mold growth, and packaging damage.

Adsorbent desiccants respond quickly to changes in humidity, making them ideal for controlled and stable environments.

Using adsorbent desiccants helps maintain dryness, protect sensitive goods, and ensure consistent storage conditions.
